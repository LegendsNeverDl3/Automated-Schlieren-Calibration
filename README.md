### What is a Schlieren
Schlieren (imaging) is an optical technique that utilizes the refraction of light caused by changes in air density, pressure and temp that typically can't be seen by the human eye.
Think for example, of heat waves that can be seen at certain angles rising from concrete in summer. Can you see heat waves rising from your hands? No. But a Schlieren imaging system can!

### Why Schlieren?
With a Schlieren, you can visualize not only heat waves, but also sound. This is because sound is fundamentally just changes in pressure in the air. So you can visualize these changes. 

##### Acoustic Levitator?
Simply(not actually that simple :)), a system that utilizes sound to levitate objects through the creation of standing waves that are built through constructive and destructive interference(sound travels in waves).

##### Why Automate?
A Schlieren imaging system requires submillimeter accuracy. Changes in temperature, wind, and humidity can all cause the imaging system to lose accuracy and give a poorer contrast image. As such, comes the necessity of an automated calibrator that can save hours of manual recalibration time!
